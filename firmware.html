Firmware updates

Upload once. Choose a device. Install when you are ready.

Connect

This key permits firmware updates on this hub, even without internet.

1. Choose your device

2. Upload firmware

Upload the sketch .bin from your Arduino build. It must retain SmartSnap OTA support. Uploading does not start installation.

3. Install

Off keeps the saved device ID, home, Wi-Fi and server. On uses the settings compiled into the new firmware after a successful update. Create the new device in the app first. The old device entry is kept.

The device restarts during installation. Completion is verified when it reconnects with the expected firmware hash.
